## 2024-05-22 - [Modal Form Accessibility]
**Learning:** Forms in modals often lack semantic structure (like `<form>` tags and `htmlFor` attributes), which compromises accessibility and standard behaviors (like submitting with Enter).
**Action:** Always wrap modal inputs in a `<form>` and ensure strict label-input association.
